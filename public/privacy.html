Privacy

Last updated 21 October 2026

Classi is a free, non-commercial fan project that turns a character idea into a Baldur’s Gate 3 build. This page explains what it stores, who else sees your data, and how to have it deleted. In short: very little is stored, nothing is sold, and there are no ads or analytics.

If you use Classi as a guest

  • Your browser is given an anonymous session (a random ID, with no name or email). Classi records when that ID generates a build, so it can enforce the daily limit.
  • Your recent builds and your session are kept in your own browser’s local storage. They never leave your device unless you sign in and save a build.

If you sign in with Google

  • Google shares your name, email address and profile picture with Classi. Classi uses these only to recognise you when you come back. Classi never sees your Google password.
  • Builds you choose to save, including the character description and any edits you make, are stored with your account so you can open them on any device.
  • As for guests, Classi records when you generate builds, to enforce the daily limit.

Your character descriptions

To create a build, the description you type is sent to OpenRouter, which passes it to an AI model run by Google AI Studio. Classi uses Google’s free tier, under which Google may use what you send to improve its products. Please don’t put anything personal or private in a character description.

Services Classi relies on

  • Supabase stores accounts, saved builds and the daily-limit records.
  • Vercel hosts the site. Like any web host, it may briefly log requests, including your IP address.
  • OpenRouter and Google AI Studio generate the builds, as described above.
  • Google handles sign-in, if you choose to use it.

Classi does not sell, rent or share your data with anyone else, and does not use it for advertising.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • You can delete any saved build yourself with the ✕ next to it.
  • To delete your account and everything stored with it, email liwanag.paulina@gmail.com from the address you signed in with. It will be removed within 30 days.
  • Clearing your browser’s site data removes anything Classi kept on your device.

Changes

If this page changes in a way that matters, the date at the top will change with it. Questions are welcome at the email address above.
